About Pawsitive Pet Academy

Pawsitive Pet Academy was established in 2023 to provide pet care education and practical animal training.

The academy is based in Durban and provides learning opportunities for pet owners, aspiring pet care professionals and animal lovers.

Our History

Since its establishment in 2023, Pawsitive Pet Academy has focused on helping people develop practical knowledge and skills for caring for animals.

The academy offers both longer programmes and shorter courses covering areas such as dog obedience, pet grooming, animal behaviour, puppy care and pet first aid.

🌱 Our Vision

To contribute to a community where people have the knowledge and confidence to care for animals responsibly.

🎯 Our Mission

To provide practical and responsible education in pet care, animal training and pet-related skills.

⭐ Our Goals

Our goal is to develop practical skills, promote positive animal handling and support people interested in pet-related careers and businesses.

Who We Serve

Our courses are suitable for:

  • Pet owners
  • Families
  • Pet sitters
  • Kennel staff
  • Veterinary assistants
  • Aspiring entrepreneurs
  • Animal lovers
